Skills & More
By CryptoLogiq — for WoW Forever

Previously known as Skills Already Known.

Know what to learn, compare spell ranks and make informed training choices as you level. Skills & More brings spell, talent, role and profession-learning information together with clear visual markers.

LEARN AT A GLANCE

• NEW +: learnable now.
• Green check: already learned.
• NEXT: the next learning milestone.
• NOT NOW: not yet available.

Colored outlines make trainer entries easier to distinguish. NEW + can also glow. Profession trainers highlight the next training step with NEXT.

UNDERSTAND YOUR SPELL RANKS

• BEST: the highest rank you have learned.
• LOW: an older rank you have already replaced.
• BUY NEW RANK: an accessible higher rank remains to learn.
• ECO: rank 1 can suffice for the recommended use while leveling.
• OPTIONAL: a spell or extra rank whose purchase can wait for your selected roles.

ECO concerns training gold, not guaranteed mana savings. OPTIONAL does not mean useless. Advice covers selected spells and must suit every role you choose; selecting PvP suspends ECO and OPTIONAL. Hover recognized spells for explanations, including on action bars. Upgrade markers do not check whether you can afford the price.

FIND WHAT YOU JUST LEARNED

RECENT marks your latest learned spells, talent ranks and recipes in supported views. The complete list is available in Apprentissages récents and stays saved between sessions.

A trainer visit without learning anything keeps the previous list. New learning replaces it. On first use, already-known spells from your latest accessible level tier are selected when their required levels are available.

GET TO KNOW YOUR SPECIALIZATION

Choose among the roles available to your class and read specialization summaries with advantages and drawbacks. The first-use window suggests a specialization from your talents or the solo leveling recommendation; advice becomes active only after you confirm.

Role labels appear in the talent trees and spellbook. LEVELING marks the main solo recommendation. PVP and PVE PVP explain the suggested use of selected talents and learnable spells. Before level 10, spellbook school tabs also offer specialization summaries.

RECOGNIZE BOOKS AND RECIPES

Warlock pet grimoires show learned or learnable status and demon icons at merchants and in bags. Merchants also indicate books you already own. Summon each demon to record its learned spells.

ALREADY KNOWN identifies learned recipes in bags and group or raid loot-roll windows when the game confirms them. The addon never chooses a loot roll for you.

MAKE IT YOURS

Use /sam settings or the minimap button to open the options. Control icons by display zone, and toggle outlines, text and backgrounds separately. Settings are saved per character.

The launcher is also available through HidingBar and Titan Panel. The options include recent learning, patchnotes, reset confirmations and copyable support reports.

COMPATIBILITY

Bag markers have been tested with Blizzard bags and EllesmereUI Bags. Other bag addons and custom profession windows are not confirmed.

DarkMode and TrainerSpells have dedicated compatibility handling. TrainerSpells keeps control of its trainer text; its class list can show purchase advice, usage markers and RECENT where space allows. Higher-rank purchase markers use TrainerSpells or information from a trainer you have opened.

COMMANDS

/sam — summary and refresh
/sam settings — options
/sam debug — trainer report
/sam bookdebug — spellbook report
/sam pet — saved pet spells
/sam petdebug — pet grimoire report
/sam conflicts — compatibility report

For a bug report, open the affected window, reproduce the issue and share the relevant Copy Export report.
