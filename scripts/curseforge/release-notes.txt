Version 0.4.0

Skills Already Known is now Skills & More — By CryptoLogiq.
The main command is now /sam.

NEW FEATURES

• Trainer markers: NEW +, learned check, NEXT and NOT NOW, with colored outlines and an optional NEW + glow.
• Spellbook rank markers: BEST, LOW and BUY NEW RANK.
• Role-based ECO and OPTIONAL training advice, with explanations in spell tooltips.
• RECENT markers and a saved list of your latest learned spells, talents and recipes. First use can highlight known spells from your latest accessible level tier.
• ALREADY KNOWN markers for learned recipes in bags and group or raid loot-roll windows.
• Class-appropriate role and specialization choices, confirmed before advice becomes active.
• Specialization summaries, advantages, drawbacks and leveling recommendations.
• Role labels, LEVELING recommendations and selected PVP / PVE PVP markers.
• Purchase advice, usage markers and RECENT in the TrainerSpells class list where space allows.
• Separate display zones and controls for icons, outlines, text and backgrounds.
• A minimap button, HidingBar / Titan Panel launcher, reset confirmations and in-game patchnotes.

BUG FIXES

• Unlearned and future-level ranks no longer cause incorrect LOW markers.
• Eligible unranked spells can receive OPTIONAL advice, including Eye of Kilrogg.
• Spellbook markers hide when switching to another addon's tab.
• Specialization labels remain recognized when DarkMode recolors titles.
• TrainerSpells list markers follow the correct spells while scrolling.
• The next profession training step shows NEXT with its yellow background.
• Talent usage markers and RECENT sit below icons to keep their images and spent points visible.
• Reset and first-use confirmation buttons work correctly; dismissing the first-use window no longer repeatedly reopens it during play.
